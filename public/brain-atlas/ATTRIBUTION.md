# Attribution and use

## 3D meshes and base hierarchy

The region meshes in `surface.glb` and `internal.glb`, and the hierarchy in
`atlas.json`, were extracted from the **Neurotorium 3D Brain Atlas**:

- https://neurotorium.org/tool/brain-atlas/
- Neurotorium is an educational platform from the Lundbeck Foundation.

They are included here as a **research / portfolio prototype**. They are not
claimed as original work. Before using this site commercially or redistributing
the models, obtain permission from the rights holder or replace the meshes with
an openly licensed atlas (for example, an Allen Human Brain Atlas-derived mesh
or a FreeSurfer/MNI surface released under a compatible licence).

## Persian content

The Persian names, short notes, reading layers, interface copy and code in this
repository are original to this project. The English descriptions from the
source dataset are **not** shipped in the page; `atlas.json` contains only the
structure names, Latin synonyms and the original Persian summaries in
`scripts/brain-atlas-fa.mjs`.
